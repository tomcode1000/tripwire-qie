§ 01THE PROBLEM

It's not if your key leaks. It's when.

A .env pushed by mistake. A key pasted into the wrong chat. A laptop with malware. Bots search for private keys around the clock, and they're faster than you. By the time you notice, the wallet is empty and you have no idea where it leaked.

§ 02HOW IT WORKS

The bot's speed is what catches it.

Scammers use fake "free money" wallets to trap greedy people. We flipped the trick. Your bait looks like a real key with money in it. A bot grabs it first, and that's your alarm, while your real key is still safe.

  1. 01

    Hide the bait

    Make fake keys in your browser and hide them where real keys live: your repo's .env, your notes, your CI secrets.

  2. 02

    Carry on building

    Nothing changes for you. Planting bait costs next to nothing on QIE: a transaction is about $0.000004.

  3. 03

    A bot bites

    The bait balance can't be moved. Any attempt to move it sets off the trap, inside the thief's own transaction.

  4. 04

    You're covered

    Your funds lock, you're told which file leaked, and the thief's address goes on the blocklist.

§ 03YOUR FUNDS

Even with your real key, a thief has to wait 24 hours.

Your real money sits in a Tripwire vault. Small amounts go out instantly. Anything bigger waits 24 hours, and you can cancel it. Only your backup key, the one you keep offline, can change the rules.

Banks have put time locks on vault doors for a hundred years. Same idea: knowing the code isn't enough.

§ 05THE BLOCKLIST

One catch protects everyone on QIE.

Only a trap can add an address, inside the thief's own transaction, so nobody gets blocklisted by rumour. Any wallet, app or AI agent can check an address before it pays.

Is this address safe to pay?
// in a contract: refuse to pay a caught thief require(!IFlagRegistry(BLOCKLIST).isFlagged(to), "blocklisted"); // in an app or AI agent if (await blocklist.isFlagged(payee)) refuse();

§ 06STRAIGHT ANSWERS

What Tripwire won't do.

As of —: live on QIE testnet, — automated tests passing, not audited yet. Mainnet is next.

  • Catch a thief who never touches the baitNo trap fires. The 24-hour wait on big withdrawals is what protects you then, so keep your real funds in the vault.
  • Protect real money in a bait walletThe fake balance can't move. Anything real you put there can. Keep bait wallets empty.
  • Move your money for youThe watcher that catches native-coin sweeps can only lock and blocklist. It can never send funds anywhere.
  • Inflate our numbersBait wallets are tools, not users. We don't count them as traction.
